A little help,
right where you type.

Good Prompts is a Chrome extension for rough prompts in ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

01

Type your rough prompt.

Start in the chat box, just as you normally would.

02

Click once to rewrite it.

Click the extension icon, or press Option + Shift + R on Mac (Alt + Shift + R on Windows).

03

Review it. You're in charge.

The clearer prompt replaces yours in place. A small note says what was guessed. Click again to restore your original. You press send.

Why a better prompt
is worth it.

The answer you get is only as good as the prompt you send. Good Prompts writes the good one for you.

01

Say less.

Type one rough line, the way you think. You don't have to spell everything out or learn how to write prompts.

02

Spend less.

A clear prompt needs fewer retries and follow-ups, so you use fewer messages and tokens to get what you wanted.

03

Prompt better.

The AI is told the task, who it's for, the format and the length. You get a useful answer the first time, not a vague one to fix.

After one click

The first example is written by hand. Type your own rough prompt and press Rewrite to try the real thing, a few times a day. Your text goes to Good Prompts and its AI model to write the rewrite, and is not stored.

Clarity, not padding.

A better prompt doesn't need to be a longer performance.

01

It never invents facts.

If only you know a missing detail, it leaves a blank like [date it broke] for you to fill in.

02

The guesses are yours to change.

It guesses only audience, format, length and tone, and writes each guess into the prompt in plain words.

03

It knows when to leave things alone.

Clear prompts and short follow-ups like “make it shorter” stay untouched. No “You are an expert” openers. One line becomes two to four sentences, not a page.
