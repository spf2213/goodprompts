Privacy policy

What we keep,
and why.

This page covers the Good Prompts website and its early-access waitlist. The short version: if you join the waitlist we keep your email address, and we use it only to write to you about Good Prompts.

Who runs this site

This site and the Good Prompts Chrome extension are run by Good Prompts. You can reach us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you submit it in the waitlist form, and the date and time you joined.
  • Your network address, briefly. To stop automated sign-ups, the server counts sign-up attempts by IP address. That count is held in memory for about ten minutes and is never saved with your email.
  • Standard hosting records. The site is hosted by Vercel, and the waitlist is stored in a database run by Neon. Vercel may log routine request details such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed, and may show us overall visit numbers.

The site's own code sets no cookies and runs no advertising trackers. The page loads its typeface from Google Fonts and its hands image from Higgsfield, so your browser makes a request to each of those services when the page opens.

What we use it for

We use your email address to tell you when early access opens and to send occasional updates about Good Prompts. We do not sell it, rent it or use it for anything else.

Who else sees it

Your email address is stored with the companies that host this site and its database, which handle it on our behalf. If we start using a service to send emails, it will handle your address for that purpose only. We will share information if the law requires it. Your information may be stored on servers outside the country you live in.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until the project ends, whichever comes first.

Your choices

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] to be removed from the waitlist, to correct your address, or to ask what we hold about you. We will act on it within 30 days. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your information, and you can use the same address to exercise them.

The extension is separate

The Good Prompts extension is not publicly available yet, and the waitlist form collects no prompts. Before the extension is released it will get its own privacy policy, covering what it reads and where that text goes.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their email addresses.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date below.
